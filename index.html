Seven Kanda pathways

Navigate the epic by narrative stage, characters, places and themes. Each pathway is designed to expand into source-aware reading, audio and learning modules.

🌅
பால காண்டம்

Bala Kanda

Origins, teachers, vows, childhood and Sita’s marriage.

Focus: beginnings, learning, discipline and dharma.

👑
அயோத்தியா காண்டம்

Ayodhya Kanda

Kingship, exile, sacrifice, family duty and Bharata’s devotion.

Focus: duty, truth, restraint and responsibility.

🌲
அரண்ய காண்டம்

Aranya Kanda

Forest life, turning points, Jatayu and Shabari.

Focus: vigilance, loss, courage and faith.

🐒
கிஷ்கிந்தா காண்டம்

Kishkindha Kanda

Alliance, friendship, Sugriva and the search mission.

Focus: promises, teamwork and purposeful action.

🔥
சுந்தர காண்டம்

Sundara Kanda

Hanuman’s leap, search, courage and meeting with Sita.

Focus: devotion, service, hope and perseverance.

🏹
யுத்த காண்டம்

Yuddha Kanda

Rama Setu, battle, justice, reunion and return.

Focus: conflict, consequences and restoration.

🪷
உத்தர காண்டம்

Uttara Kanda

Later traditions, kingship, legacy and complex interpretive questions.

Focus: source context, textual tradition and careful interpretation.
