# SitaRama Legacy Content Scope

The SitaRama legacy website preserves RamaVerse's Ramayana knowledge base while presenting the web identity around Sri Rama and Sita together.

## Priority content
- Sri Rama and Sita profiles
- Seven Kandas and major narrative milestones
- Hanuman, Lakshmana, Bharata and major/minor characters
- Ayodhya, Mithila, forests, Kishkindha, Setu and Lanka geography
- Dharma, leadership, family, courage and life-guidance themes
- Children’s stories, quizzes and audio learning
- Temple/pilgrimage context where reliably sourced

## Controls
- Preserve source labels and direct-verse/summary/reflection distinctions.
- Preserve confidence tags and AI hallucination guardrails.
- Do not make guaranteed remedy, job, marriage, health or wealth claims.
- Keep package ID and mobile release identity unchanged.
