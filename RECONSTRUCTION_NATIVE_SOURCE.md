# RamaVerse Native Reconstruction Contract

## Authority
Recovered Manus source is the next reconstruction authority. The native app exposes dedicated RamaVerse routes including Kandas, Wisdom, Characters, Places, Guidance, Stories, Quizzes, Audio, Search, Ask RamaVerse, Intelligence, Library, Journey, Timeline, Knowledge Graph, Rama Life, Walk With Rama, Experience Center, Sarga Reader and reconciliation/owner workspaces. It also contains a dedicated `shared/visualUniverse.ts` and motion primitives.

## No-donor UI rule
Do not use DivyaNexus, Kandan, SitaRama, KirthiVerse or OmSaravanaBhava as visual/layout donors. Donor material may be consulted only for verified missing content, rollback or provenance evidence.

## Native design target
Cinematic Ramayana world, seven-Kanda journey, character constellations, sacred geography, source-aware reading and bounded AI discovery. Preserve the native RamaVerse visual universe and motion language rather than applying a generic dark dashboard or devotional card template.

## Required gates before main
- Native source builds from clean install.
- Core routes render and navigate without dead controls.
- Desktop, tablet and mobile visual QA.
- Motion has reduced-motion fallback.
- Accessibility/keyboard/focus semantics pass.
- Rights/provenance for visual assets are explicit.
- No cross-site duplication of hero, nav, card grammar, background treatment or motion grammar.
- Existing production remains rollback-safe until exact candidate is verified.

## Release rule
No production cutover from this branch until a source-fidelity screenshot comparison and route/function evidence are attached to the PR.
